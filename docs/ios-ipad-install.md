# Gold POS on iPhone and iPad

Gold POS can be installed from Safari as a **Home Screen web app (PWA)**. It is not an App Store download or a separate native iOS application.

## Install

1. Open the secure site in **Safari**: <https://goldpos.shwetaungnyunt.tech/>. The old `http://` address redirects to HTTPS; HTTPS is required for the secure web-app experience.
2. Tap Safari's **Share** button (the square with an upward arrow).
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. Open the new **Gold POS** icon and sign in with the existing employee account.

On iPad, the Share button may be in the top toolbar. If **Add to Home Screen** is not visible, scroll the share sheet or check Safari's **Edit Actions** list.

## What to expect

- The app opens in a standalone window from the Home Screen and adapts to portrait or landscape orientation.
- iPhone/iPad safe areas and touch-sized form controls are supported.
- An internet connection is required. This release intentionally does not cache POS records or provide offline transaction entry.
- Employee accounts and permissions are the same as the website.
- This PWA does not require App Store review or an Apple Developer account. A native App Store app would be a separate project.
